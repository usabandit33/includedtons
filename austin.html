Ask before you pay

  1. Is this your truck, or a broker?
  2. How many tons are included?
  3. What is overage per ton, and the extra-day fee?
  4. If this sits on the street, who files the TURP permit?
  5. Do you take a card on your own account — not Zelle?

Permit in one minute

Your driveway: usually no City of Austin permit. Street, sidewalk, or alley: TURP through Austin Build + Connect, often about $45 plus a small surcharge and a few business days. Confirm on the city portal before the truck uses the parking lane.

Advertised Austin 20-yard prices often sit in the mid-$400s to mid-$500s before overage. Roofing and dirt can blow a 1-ton cap while the can still looks half empty.
